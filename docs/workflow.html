From your documents to any application form

Six diagrams of what actually runs: which services talk to each other, what happens to an uploaded document, how a form gets answered, how the extension works on another website, and how a password reset travels through email.

1. What talks to what

The React app only ever calls the FastAPI backend. The backend owns every outside connection, so keys and passwords never reach the browser.

2. Your journey through the app

Build the profile once, then every application starts from it. Three steps are always yours: settling conflicts, reviewing answers, and approving.

3. What happens to an uploaded document

Each upload runs through five recorded steps. The names in code font are the step names you see under Documents → Processing pipeline.

4. How a form field gets its answer

Retrieval-augmented generation, with a check at the end: an answer survives only if it can be traced to your profile or to a passage from your own documents.

5. Filling a form on any website

The Chrome/Edge extension works from what each field means, not from site-specific code. It sends only field metadata, fills only what you approve, and never presses Submit. The in-app CareerHub demo runs the same code.

6. Forgot password, by email

The reset link exists only in the email. After changing the password you sign in again, and every other session ends.

What protects your data along the way

Encrypted at rest

Files, extracted values, passages, applications, templates and saved answers are encrypted before they are stored. Vectors stay readable so the database can search them.

AI is checked

LLM output must be found in your documents or profile. Smart Answers are always suggestions, and conflicting values are never filled for you.

Sessions

httpOnly cookie, scrypt-hashed passwords, Remember me for 30 days, and a password change signs out every other device.

Extension stays in its lane

A token only for autofill, profile, documents and Smart Answers. Sends field labels and options, never page text or what you typed. Your own name never goes into “Father’s Name”.

Nothing is submitted for you

Approval marks an application ready. On other websites the extension fills only what you approve, and you press Submit.
